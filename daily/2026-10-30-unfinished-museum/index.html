Curator's note / Why this exists

In praise of the almost.

This isn't an actual museum. It's a six-piece graphic art collection presented using the visual language of a gallery exhibition.

Each illustrated piece exaggerates an ordinary feeling into a fictional object: procrastination becomes a broken clock, regret becomes an impossible map, and needing praise becomes an applause machine. The humour lives in the gap between ordinary human frustrations and the grand language of a formal museum.

How to explore the posters: Choose any exhibit to open its story and curatorial label. Print a label as a souvenir of something that never existed.
