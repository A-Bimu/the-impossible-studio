01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW
THE IMPOSSIBLE STUDIO — CASE STUDY

The idea

CHERRY RIOT is a fictional premium jam brand and immersive product website exploring how storytelling, motion and texture can turn an everyday product into a memorable digital experience.

The goal

✓ A distinct, playful product identity

✓ An engaging scroll-driven website

✓ Realistic product-context visuals

✓ Responsive, working shopping interactions
