## New approval checklist

1. Identify which exact render, webpage, or file was approved.
2. Preserve the approved original before iterating.
3. Place it in the appropriate project folder with an accurate README.
4. Remove secrets and personally identifying content before publishing.
5. Confirm the asset or app is truly visible in GitHub, and update this registry.
