01 / The problem

Why this
exists.

Clinic booking can feel like navigating a directory rather than getting help. People need to understand services, choose the right practitioner, see time choices and know what they have booked.

02 / The design response

Intentional
by design.

The new experience gives photography and editorial storytelling room to breathe, while deep berry and raspberry support recognisable calls to action. Doctor imagery is illustrative stock photography, not photographs of the fictional named clinicians.

05 / Honest prototype notes

What is
really built.

VELA HEALTH is not a real clinic. Clinicians, services and appointments are fictional; portrait imagery is illustrative licensed stock photography, not the fictional clinicians named in the demo. No real booking is created, no medical records are kept, and no advice or treatment is offered. Bookings save only in this browser.

This is independent speculative portfolio work, not a commissioned client project. All eight device previews display responsive states of the actual HTML and JavaScript demo. The interactions can be tested in a full-screen browser. The case-study presentation pairs original coded brand artwork with illustrative Unsplash-licensed real photography. Photographed people do not represent or endorse these fictional businesses. No completed videos or live services are claimed.
