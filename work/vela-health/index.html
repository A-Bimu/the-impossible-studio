VELA
HEALTH.

Healthcare should feel less complicated. A warm, vivid digital experience designed to turn uncertain first steps into a clear, human care journey.

A little less searching.

Most booking experiences expect people to know where to begin. VELA makes the first choice approachable, before asking for dates or personal details.

A lot more clarity.

The service someone chooses determines their sample clinician options. The form keeps each decision visible, and the saved visit can be reviewed or cancelled.

Warm. Clear.
Human.

Deep violet creates confidence, lilac gives the layout breathing room, and yellow brings warmth to the important decisions. Bold, generously spaced typography replaces fussy captions and decorative subheadings.

Designed to make
the next step obvious.

The creative process starts with the visitor's real tasks, not a stack of pretty screens.

Discover.

Explore clear, uncomplicated services and decide where to begin.

Connect.

Find fictional clinicians that match the selected specialty.

Plan.

Choose a sample time and format, with a readable summary before confirmation.

Stay in control.

Review or cancel the saved sample visit, and see its slot become available again.

Take it for
a spin.

This is a real interactive frontend demo, not eight screenshots of the same homepage. Choose a task, change between laptop and phone, then expand the working interface.

Good design should
work, too.

VELA HEALTH is original speculative portfolio work, not a commissioned clinic project. All clinicians, services, availability and appointments are fictional. Photographs are illustrative stock images, not pictures of the named clinicians. No medical advice is provided, no real booking is made, and sample bookings are stored only in this browser.

The photography is used under the Unsplash license. No real healthcare service is claimed.
