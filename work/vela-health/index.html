01 / The problem

Why this
exists.

Most clinic booking journeys begin with decisions people are expected to understand already. This speculative experience starts with plain-language service choices, explains the next step and brings the appointment into view.

02 / The design response

Intentional
by design.

Warm paper, bold raspberry, apricot accents and expressive editorial typography make the homepage unmistakably VELA. The care-picker, matching clinician directory and appointment flow now behave as one connected experience—not unrelated demo pages.

03 / Creative process

From idea
to interface.

01 / Empathy

Start with reassurance

Explain how to get started without making visitors understand medical terminology first.

02 / Navigation

Keep the journey legible

Match the selected service to a fictional clinician, then let the visitor choose a sample visit format and time.

03 / Completion

Confirmation should be certain

Sample booking times cannot be booked twice in the same browser, confirmation reflects the chosen visit format, and cancellations restore that local time.

06 / FROM A QUESTION TO A PLAN

A better first
five minutes.

Four connected interactions, designed for the moments when people just want to know what happens next. Every step is explorable in the working prototype below.

01 — DISCOVERStart with everyday language.

A small set of care options replaces overwhelming category lists.

02 — CONNECTKeep clinician choices relevant.

The selected specialty determines the sample clinicians offered.

03 — PLANMake appointment choices visible.

A clear summary tracks clinician, date, format and sample time.

04 — CONTROLChange plans without starting over.

Cancel a local sample appointment and see its time become available again.

06 / Honest prototype notes

What is
really built.

VELA HEALTH is not a real clinic. Clinicians, services and appointments are fictional; portrait imagery is illustrative licensed stock photography, not the fictional clinicians named in the demo. No real booking is created, no medical records are kept, and no advice or treatment is offered. Bookings save only in this browser.

This is independent speculative portfolio work, not a commissioned client project. The case study offers one interactive laptop-or-phone experience with expandable, working product journeys, rather than repeating the homepage in eight small frames. The case-study presentation pairs original coded brand artwork with illustrative Unsplash-licensed real photography. Photographed people do not represent or endorse these fictional businesses. No completed videos or live services are claimed.
