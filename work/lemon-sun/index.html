The Lemon Sun.

An illustrated escape into endless summer.

Plate one: the approved artwork, mounted at its own proportions. Page two is a separate file, the botanical story page, where the same words are set as live type.
Plate 01 / The approved artwork900 x 1125 · untouched
01 / The piece

A town that is one fruit.

A surreal editorial artwork about what happens when a familiar fruit becomes the atmosphere of an entire sunlit world. The lemon slice is drawn at the scale of a sun and the sea below it stays ceramic blue, so the whole scene reads as painted tile rather than as weather.

What existsOne approved artwork, presented above at native size with no enlargement and no recolouring.
What is addedA companion page that sets the artwork's own story as live, selectable editorial text.
What is conceptualNo motion video, live site or printed edition has been produced for this piece.
02 / Presentation

Two pages, not one.

The approved direction is a main artwork plus a separate editorial companion, and the two are deliberately separate files rather than one merged image. This page is page one: the artwork on its own, at the size it was drawn. Page two is the botanical story page, which sets the words the poster already carries as live type so they can be read, enlarged and selected instead of being locked inside a picture.

The companion editorial

The same artwork, read as a magazine spread: the story set in live type, a drop cap, a pull quote, and four detail plates cropped from the original illustration so the craft can be inspected up close.
