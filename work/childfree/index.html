03 / Making a set like this

Designed to be worn.

The collection was drawn around the limits of hard enamel rather than around a screen. Those limits are what keep nine different ideas readable as physical objects, and they are as much a part of the design as the typography.

  • Scale. Each pin sits between 22 and 25 mm across, small enough that nine designs can share one card and still sit together on a jacket.
  • Finish. Hard enamel, polished flat, on a gold-plated outline. The outline carries every edge, which is why the lettering can stay thin and still hold.
  • Colour. Four enamel wells per pin at most. Fewer wells means a cleaner read at size and a simpler die to cut.
  • Lettering. One statement per pin, held to a single line wherever the silhouette allows. Below roughly 4 mm cap height the letters stop working in enamel.
  • Hardware. Flat pins use a single butterfly clutch. The pin with a dangling earring charm needs a reinforced jump ring instead.
  • Making. Nothing has been quoted, sampled or manufactured yet. These are the production decisions the artwork assumes, not a purchase.
