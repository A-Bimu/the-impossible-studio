Wearable identity / Enamel pins

Small pins.
Loud choices.

Childfree, Unapologetically is a set of nine playful enamel-pin concepts about deciding what a complete life looks like for yourself. Three moods, one point of view.

Browse the individual concepts below. Each close-up is a view of the original approved artwork, not a new rendering or manufactured product.

02 / Explore the collection

Pick a personality.

Whimsical softness, everyday calm or bold statements. Select any pin to examine the original illustration more closely.

9 of 9 concepts
03 / Design intent

Designed to be worn, not just read.

Banner silhouettes, expressive type and metal-outline details are the visual language. The concept sheet tests nine different ways to make a personal statement at pin scale.

04 / What is real

A design study, not a storefront.

These are original illustrated concepts. No physical inventory, manufacturing, orders or payments exist. The nine close-ups use the original sheet as their only image source, without substituting generated artwork.
