AGRI AI.

What if asking about your crops felt as simple as asking someone who understands your farm?

A fictional, research-led agricultural decision-support platform for Kenyan smallholders, emerging farmers and extension partners. The experience combines crop observation, stage-based planning and honest market calculations without presenting invented results as facts.

01 / WHAT WE STUDIED

START WITH
THE FIELD.

We started with real agricultural advisory products instead of inventing a generic “AI for farming” feature list. PlantVillage Nuru demonstrates offline crop-disease image recognition; Farmer.Chat shows crop- and location-aware advisory; FarmerAI shows why Kenya's mobile, market and weather context matters.

These findings suggest that an agricultural product should ask about the user's crop, symptoms, stage and location before offering useful next steps. They do not prove a proposed interface improves yields or replaces an agronomist.

02 / THE PROBLEM

MORE SIGNAL.
LESS GUESSING.

The real UX challenge isn't adding five AI badges. It's helping a farmer move through an uncertain situation: observe → describe → understand options → check with a professional → take action.

We kept three jobs central: identify what needs attention, keep a practical plan, and make sense of selling numbers. Each workflow has a clear start, result and next step.

Large touch targets, language that avoids unexplained agronomy jargon, responsive design and honest uncertainty are priorities. Future real-world research should test offline access, voice and Kiswahili with farmers before claiming these improve usability.

03 / THE VISUAL IDENTITY

NO MORE
GENERIC
AGRITECH.

We intentionally avoided pale cream cards and the automatic “make everything green” approach to agricultural branding. Instead, deep midnight and cobalt create a clear digital foundation, with sunflower yellow for action, paprika orange for emphasis and violet for selected graphics.

Real field photography does the human work. Utility-first forms handle the actual interaction. The site makes a confident impression without obscuring meaning.

06 / WHAT'S REAL AND WHAT ISN'T

HONEST BY
DESIGN.

The frontend, responsive layouts, photo preview, sample crop triage, stage-based checklist, browser-local task persistence and sample market calculator are coded interactions. They do not constitute a trained disease model, current weather forecasting, real market intelligence or professional diagnosis. No crop photo is transmitted to a server by this frontend. There is no user account, live API or back-end storage.
