03 / The concept

A calmer
operating system.

A professional Deep Navy exploration of multi-industry management software. Visual hierarchy, restrained colour and dense-but-readable dashboards create an operational system with a calmer rhythm. The same shell adapts to a retail shop, a salon, a restaurant or a services business, so the working day starts in one place instead of five.

What existsWorking HTML prototype: overview, sell, transactions, customers, inventory, expenses, team, reports and settings
What worksRecording a sale, adding stock and customers, switching business templates, exporting the demo ledger
What's conceptualFictional Kenyan business data; no M-PESA or bank connection and no production database
04 / The decisions

Information
first.

Instead of dressing business software in unrelated decoration, the design puts the information architecture first. Charts, navigation and actions are visually distinct without taking attention away from operational tasks. Money is stated plainly in KES, pending mobile payments are separated from confirmed cash, and the mobile layout collapses to a bottom bar rather than shrinking the desktop sidebar.

ColourDeep Navy, the approved colourway, with a warm sand accent
ResponsiveDesktop sidebar becomes a bottom tab bar on small screens
HonestyEvery screen is labelled demo data; no real charge is possible
05 / Provenance

What is
real here.

What exists: the working Deep Navy HTML prototype embedded above, plus the approved desktop and mobile previews in the collection folder.
What is conceptual: the business, its staff, its customers and its money. The prototype is a design demonstration and is not connected to a commercial database or payment provider.
