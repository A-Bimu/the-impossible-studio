ONE.

One place for the moving parts of a business.

From the studio archive. Presented in its actual medium, with an honest account of what exists and what remains conceptual.
Actual archived artwork / Original presentationProduct interface / Management systems
01 / The concept

A professional Deep Navy exploration of multi-industry management software. Visual hierarchy, restrained colour and dense-but-readable dashboards create an operational system with a calmer rhythm.

02 / The decisions

Instead of dressing business software in unrelated decoration, the design puts the information architecture first. Charts, navigation and actions are visually distinct without taking attention away from operational tasks.

03 / Provenance

What exists: Working HTML dashboard prototype and approved desktop/mobile previews.
What's conceptual: Business metrics and contents in the prototype are illustrative, not connected to a commercial database.
