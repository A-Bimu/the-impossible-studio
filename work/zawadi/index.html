01 / Brand idea

A financial partner
for what's next.

Money shapes everyday decisions. The brand should make those moments feel easier, not heavier. This identity was designed around confidence, openness and the freedom to move forward.

A simpler way to move through money.

The starting point wasn't a banking dashboard. It was a feeling: people should be able to see what they have, move it when they need to, and focus on the bigger picture.

Instead of a loud technology aesthetic, I chose a calmer look that works equally well in a serious financial setting and in someone's everyday life.

02 / Logo and identity

A mark made
to move.

Two forward-leaning forms create a compact symbol. It works on app icons and cards, with a simple wordmark that doesn't compete for attention.

Zawadi.Primary brand lockup · Dark
Zawadi.Symbol and wordmark · Light

The thinking
The two shapes suggest forward motion and connection. They are abstract enough to remain flexible across identity, advertising and product design.

The tone
Modern, grounded and human. Warm neutrals balance the confidence of deep green. Every element has a job.

03 / Color & type

A palette that
feels considered.

Deep green anchors the identity. Stone ivory creates breathing room, while soft sage, slate and warm copper add subtle contrast. The result feels calm without becoming forgettable.
