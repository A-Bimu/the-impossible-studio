01 / The idea

Money is personal.

People don't need another complicated way to manage it.

The brief was to make finance feel straightforward without stripping away personality. A restrained palette, generous space and confident typography create something both approachable and trustworthy.

So instead of beginning with a banking dashboard, I started with the feeling the brand should leave behind: more room to do what matters.

Positioning / The thought behind the brand
Less money admin.
More life.
02 / The visual identity

A mark with
room to move.

Two open forms meet without closing into a perfect circle. It's a simple idea about connection and possibility, designed to hold its own on a card, an icon or a full-size campaign.

Not a coin.

04 / A little perspective

See patterns, not just payments.

A simple spending view helps turn a statement into an understandable story. The same brand colors now organize information rather than merely decorating it.

06 / What makes the experience work

Expressive on the outside.
Precise where it matters.

Understand before sending.

Review recipients, exchange rates and fees before confirming a transfer.

Recover without guessing.

Make failed, pending and completed states distinct. Never suggest blindly retrying an uncertain transfer.

Privacy is part of the design.

Balance visibility, secure sign-in and card controls should be easy to find.
