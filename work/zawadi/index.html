01 / The thinking

Everyday money.
Less everyday friction.

A finance app is more than a list of transactions. It is where people manage bills, support family, plan for tomorrow and deal with unexpected moments. The design has to earn trust at every step.

THE CHALLENGE

Too much to navigate.

Even familiar tasks can feel fragmented when paying, sending and saving live in separate journeys.

THE IDEA

One calmer place.

Put the most common actions within immediate reach without burying useful secondary tools.

THE EXPERIENCE

Confidence by design.

Clear confirmation, explicit fees, readable receipts and recovery options make important actions easier to understand.

Design intent: Zawadi is a fictional product-design exercise. The content and transactions shown are illustrative, not live banking data or claims of an operating financial institution.
Not just an app that moves money. An app that helps people feel in control of it.
02 / Visual identity

Warm enough for people.

06 / Unhappy paths matter

Design for the moments
that go wrong.

Financial products demand unusually careful error handling. A beautiful confirmation screen means little if the product cannot explain what happened when payment status is uncertain.

⌁

No connection

Explain that the app is offline. Label cached information, offer a retry, and do not imply that a transfer was processed.

!

Insufficient balance

Show the available balance and the shortfall, including fees, with a clear path back to editing the amount.

↻

Payment pending

Display a pending reference and let the user check its status. Avoid suggesting another payment before reconciliation.

×

Transfer failed

Explain the failure, whether funds moved, and the safest next action. A help link should reference the attempted transaction.

⌛

Session expired

Preserve only safe, non-sensitive context. Prompt secure re-authentication and never retain a PIN in an exposed field.

⚙

Service unavailable

Say that the service is temporarily unavailable. Identify affected functions and provide a safe way to return.

?

Page not found

Offer navigation back to the dashboard rather than a dead end or a technical error dump.

◇

Identity review

Explain what is being reviewed, whether functionality is limited and where to find further support.

07 / Behind the interface

Designed to earn trust,
not just attention.

Beautiful screens are the visible layer. The more important layer is behavior that makes financial information clear, predictable and accessible.

01 / Confirmation before commitment

Every consequential payment includes a review step: recipient, amount, fees and total.

02 / Clear distinctions between states

Pending, failed and successful transactions must look and read differently; receipts only confirm verified outcomes.

03 / Privacy by default

Provide balance visibility controls, session security and careful handling of sensitive account details.

04 / Access for more people

Design for small screens, readable text, keyboard and screen-reader support, slow connections and plain language.

05 / Consistency throughout the system

Shared spacing, typography, buttons, form validation and system messaging keep every feature recognizable.

06 / Local context, without shortcuts

KSh currency formatting and phone-first flows reflect the intended market, without claiming regulatory approval or payment connectivity.

Concept scope: identity, user journeys, representative interfaces and experience principles. Production use would require actual provider integrations, consent and KYC processes, regulatory and security reviews, accessibility testing and real-user validation. This page does not represent a working wallet.
