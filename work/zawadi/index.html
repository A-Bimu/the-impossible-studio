04 / THE STORY

Why it's designed
this way.

The explanation belongs here, after the work. A case study should allow visitors to see the screens and visual language before being asked to read about them.

THE IDEA

Make everyday finance feel lighter.

The starting point was a simple question: how might someone understand and move their money without hunting through separate services?

THE APPROACH

One action per moment.

Balances come first. Transfers show fees before confirmation. Cards are easy to control. Error states explain the next safe step.

THE DESIGN LANGUAGE

Confident, never crowded.

Midnight navy, warm ivory, slate and copper create a recognizable personality. Clear type and spacing keep the product useful.

SECURITY & TRUST

Clarity builds confidence.

Every meaningful financial action needs understandable status, unambiguous confirmation and a recoverable failure path.

ACCESSIBILITY

Designed for real life.

Direct language, adequate contrast and consistent navigation are part of the concept, alongside slower connections and smaller screens.

PROJECT STATUS

A full design study.

Zawadi is fictional. Names, exchange rates, receipts, balances and payment cards are example content—not live accounts or issued financial products.

THE IMPOSSIBLE STUDIO / ZAWADI

More for what
moves you.

A speculative brand and full mobile-finance design experience, from onboarding to recovery. Created to explore how one cohesive identity can serve a complex everyday product.
