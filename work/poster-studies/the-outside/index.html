The approved original is shown here exactly as it was made: nothing is cropped, re-lettered or recoloured. Print outputs a single A4 portrait sheet with the artwork at maximum size; the PNG is the full-resolution file for social, slide and layout use.

Why it exists

A fictional afternoon of music, picnic culture, food and slow conversation. The event exists to make the outside feel like the main attraction.

Speculative design. The fictionally branded venue, date and prices printed inside the artwork are illustrative and are not a real event or ticket offer.

Design decisions

Who it is for
Friend groups, families and nature-loving creatives
Typography direction
DM Serif Display — chosen for its graceful and welcoming proportions. The small supporting information uses Inter for readability.
Visual idea
Generous serif lettering is balanced by bright sky colors and kite-like fabric forms. The airy layout gives viewers space to breathe.
Personality
Open · Airy · Optimistic
