An imagined gathering where DJs, visual installations and unexpected encounters take over once the city quiets down.

Design decisions

Who it is for
Night owls, sound enthusiasts, creative communities
Typography direction
Bebas Neue — chosen for tall architectural proportions. The small supporting information uses Inter for readability.
Visual idea
Tall condensed type reads like a building sign at 2 a.m. Electric cobalt and lavender beams create movement while the information stays minimal.
Personality
Electric · Moody · Nocturnal
