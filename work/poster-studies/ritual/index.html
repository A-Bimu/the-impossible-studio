Design decisions

Who it is for

Typography direction
Anton — chosen for powerful condensed lettering and immediate impact. The small supporting information uses Inter for readability.
Visual idea
Compressed monumental type carries the energy. Blurred light, restrained red and negative space evoke an encounter rather than a standard concert.
