Why it exists

An imagined daytime celebration where good food, wellness rituals, independent makers and live sounds share the same sunlit space.

Speculative design. The fictionally branded venue, date and prices printed inside the artwork are illustrative and are not a real event or ticket offer.

Design decisions

Who it is for
Curious people, friends, independent makers
Typography direction
Fraunces — chosen for its lively and approachable shapes. The small supporting information uses Inter for readability.
Visual idea
Rounded, expressive lettering pairs with hand-drawn flowers and joyful color blocks. It feels spontaneous rather than overly polished.
Personality
Joyful · Friendly · Handmade
