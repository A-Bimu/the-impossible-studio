01 / The problem

Why this
exists.

When delivery information is fragmented, every team loses time on status questions. Customers ask where a package is, while dispatch teams need to act on what happens next.

02 / The design response

Intentional
by design.

Instead of another rounded-card SaaS template, MOVA opens with a bold tracking-first logistics campaign and warehouse photography. The demo retains creation, lookup and dispatch progression against clearly fictional shipment data.

05 / Honest prototype notes

What is
really built.

MOVA uses fictional recipients and shipment routes. Maps are illustrations, not GPS or navigation. No parcels are collected or delivered, no tracking network is connected and no prices are charged. Data remains local to the browser.

This is independent speculative portfolio work, not a commissioned client project. All eight device previews display responsive states of the actual HTML and JavaScript demo. The interactions can be tested in a full-screen browser. The case-study presentation pairs original coded brand artwork with illustrative Unsplash-licensed real photography. Photographed people do not represent or endorse these fictional businesses. No completed videos or live services are claimed.
