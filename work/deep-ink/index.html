Deep Ink.

A living blue-and-white world where a creature becomes the navigation.
Original design / Concept and presentationImmersive digital design / Illustration / Motion
01 / The project

Behind the idea.

Deep Ink gives curiosity a character. A porcelain-blue octopus winds through editorial content, guiding attention to new sections, menus and moments below the surface.

What was designedOriginal illustration concepts, mobile study, interaction direction and two actual MP4 scrolling studies
What worksBoth original MP4 motion studies are archived and playable in the project section below.
What is conceptualMouse-following eyes, hover-reactive tentacles and complete website interactions are proposed behavior, not proven finished functionality.
02 / Art direction

Designed with intent.

The concept uses the creature as an interaction device instead of a decorative logo. The visual language combines pale porcelain, intense cobalt, underwater depth and carefully controlled typography.
