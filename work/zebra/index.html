ZEBRA.

NOT MADE TO BLEND IN.

A fashion brand that refuses to disappear into the crowd.
Original design / Concept and presentationFashion brand / Digital commerce / Art direction
01 / The project

Behind the idea.

The concept starts with a striking visual collision: actual zebras walking across a city zebra crossing. That familiar urban pattern becomes the language of a fashion label. Its bold first impression carries into a usable storefront.

What was designedFashion art direction, brand concept, catalogue interactions, sample bag and checkout
What worksThe original interactive fashion demo can be opened and explored. Buying is simulated.
What is conceptualFictional fashion label; all products, payments and customer accounts are illustrative.
02 / Art direction

Designed with intent.

Instead of repeating stripes as decoration, the crossing itself becomes the campaign. Stark monochrome, flashes of red and street-fashion imagery position the collection with a recognizable point of view.
