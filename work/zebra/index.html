Fashion brand / Digital commerce / Art direction

ZEBRA.

NOT MADE TO BLEND IN.

A fashion brand that refuses to disappear into the crowd. The storefront below is the approved interactive build, not a mockup of it.
Original design / Concept and presentationFashion brand / Digital commerce / Art direction
01 / The project

Behind the idea.

The concept starts with a striking visual collision: actual zebras walking across a city zebra crossing. That familiar urban pattern becomes the language of a fashion label. Its bold first impression carries into a usable storefront.

What was designedFashion art direction, brand concept, catalogue interactions, sample bag and checkout
What worksThe original interactive fashion demo can be opened and explored. Buying is simulated.
What is conceptualFictional fashion label; all products, payments and customer accounts are illustrative.
02 / The live storefront

Beyond the still.

Open full screen ↗

Browse the collection, filter it, open a product, choose a size and colour, add to the bag and run the sample checkout. Nothing is charged.

Approved interactive storefront / Original build Source: projects/zebra-fashion/

The storefront keeps its own navigation inside the frame. If the embedded view feels tight, open it full screen. Some browsers block third-party storage in frames, so saved bags and wishlists may only persist in the standalone tab.

03 / Art direction

Designed with intent.

Instead of repeating stripes as decoration, the crossing itself becomes the campaign. Stark monochrome, flashes of red and street-fashion imagery position the collection with a recognizable point of view.

04 / The experience

From campaign
to commerce.

ZEBRA is more than a dramatic campaign visual. The concept connects its art direction to an interactive fashion storefront, where visitors can explore a collection and test a simulated shopping journey. This is a design exploration, not a live store.

First impressionA visual campaign inspired by the city crossing
InteractionBrowsable collection, product options, wishlist, bag and checkout
Prototype statusInteractive demonstration with simulated purchasing
05 / Final perspective

Not made
to blend in.

ZEBRA turns one recognizable everyday symbol into a confident visual identity and an interactive digital concept. The original artwork and live demo remain the centre of this story.
