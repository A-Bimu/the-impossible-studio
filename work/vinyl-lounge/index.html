Vinyl Lounge.

A record becomes a place to meet.

From the studio archive. Presented in its actual medium, with an honest account of what exists and what remains conceptual.
Plate 01 / The approved artwork · 900 x 1599 · untouchedConcept illustration / Music culture
01 / Motion, kept restrained

Nothing here is spun.

The approved artwork in Plate 01 does not move. It is not stretched, re-coloured, wobbled or rotated to invent a film that was never made. Rotation is shown as a diagram instead: an exact drawing of the record turning, at true speed divided by ten.

33⅓ rpm · one tenth speed · 18 s per turn

The record turns, the artwork stays still.

One radial index mark is drawn on the disc, so the slow rotation is legible rather than a hidden loop. The grooves, the spindle and the fixed outer pointer are drawn from geometry in this page, not lifted from the illustration.

02

The concept

An oversized record becomes a world. The file archived here shows a turntable photographed from above, its disc covered edge to edge with a night scene: a full moon, classical ruins, ribbons, blossom and a gilded sun.

03

The decisions

Scale does the conceptual work. A familiar object is enlarged until its surface becomes landscape, and a second, smaller record sits at the centre of the collage exactly where the label would be, so the piece contains its own turntable at two scales. The night palette of crimson, bone and gold, the grain of the vinyl and the tonearm resting where it would actually sit all keep the scene reading as a real object rather than a flat graphic.

04

Provenance

What is held here: one artwork, assets/vinyl-original.webp, 900 x 1599, shown above at its native proportions and untouched. Discrepancy: the studio registry describes an approved version in which very small figures eat, drink coffee and dance across the record, and calls that the accepted visual. The file archived here shows a celestial composition instead, so one of the two records is out of date. The missing version has not been invented to fill the gap, and this page describes only what the archive actually contains. What is conceptual: no motion film, venue or interactive build has been produced, and the diagram above is a diagram, not an animated version of the illustration. Motion is a goal rather than a delivery: any animation has to be made and approved before it can be described as an animation.

05 / Status

Presented as drawn.

This project is a visual artwork, not an interactive website or video. The page adds motion around the artwork rather than motion inside it, so the approved illustration stays exactly as it was approved. If your system asks for reduced motion, every animation on this page stops and the plate appears immediately.
