Vinyl Lounge.

A record becomes a place to meet.

From the studio archive. Presented in its actual medium, with an honest account of what exists and what remains conceptual.
Actual archived artwork / Original presentationConcept illustration / Music culture
01 / The concept

An oversized vinyl record transformed into a miniature social world where people gather, eat and dance. This is an illustration concept about music as shared public space.

02 / The decisions

The scene transforms a familiar object by changing its scale and purpose. The artwork carries the concept; it should not be presented as a working app.

03 / Provenance

What exists: One approved visual artwork.
What's conceptual: No corresponding motion film or production venue has been built.

This project is a visual artwork, not an interactive website or video.
