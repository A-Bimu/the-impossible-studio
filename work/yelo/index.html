01 / RESEARCH & CONTEXT

NOT ANOTHER
BANK APP.

The starting point was Co-operative Bank Kenya's YEA youth account: a proposition created to help young people manage their finances and build entrepreneurial independence. The bank's 2008 annual report describes YEA as newly launched; the youth offer was relaunched digitally in 2024. Its published features include KES/USD account access, virtual cards, transfers, goal-based savings, mobile loans and investing from small amounts. We studied those real features—then developed a distinctly different, independent concept rather than claiming to redesign the official bank.

OBSERVATION 01

Money is personal.

Everyday transfers, bill payments, and balances should be useful before anything else. We kept the core tasks prominent and demo actions understandable.

OBSERVATION 02

Progress deserves a face.

Young people save for real goals—tuition, equipment, rent, a first business. A visual goal tracker makes the next step tangible.

DESIGN OPPORTUNITY

A financial identity with attitude.

Existing youth banking can be capable without feeling like a youth-owned brand. We combined expressive card graphics with restrained information design.

Primary product references: Co-op Bank's Young Ennovators Account and YEA feature and onboarding details. This is not an official or commissioned Co-op Bank project.

No bland gradients and no forced “tech” look. The brand pairs deep aubergine and electric purple with high-visibility orange, spray-style yellow marks and handmade lettering. We reserve the cleanest surfaces for money and financial information.

MIDNIGHT / PURPLE / HOT ORANGE / SPRAY YELLOW

The debit card is designed like a small piece of wearable culture: energetic marker strokes, bright colour, and enough whitespace to stay legible. The design is original concept artwork; it is not an issued bank or card-network product.

Inside the app, the visual language reappears in savings goals, creator content and branded card controls, while balances and transaction details remain deliberately clean.

05 / CREATIVE PROCESS

FROM BRIEF
TO BUILD.

STAGE 01 / RESEARCH

Understand the account.

Identify what young users actually need from a banking product, using the published YEA youth proposition as background.

STAGE 02 / ART DIRECTION

Find the voice.

Choose authentic photography, streetwear references, graphic marks and a graffiti-first fictional card identity.

STAGE 03 / DESIGN & CODE

Make it usable.

Implement responsive layouts, local transaction flows, a savings tracker, card controls and accessible feedback without collecting banking credentials.

06 / HONEST PROTOTYPE NOTES

WHAT'S REAL.
WHAT'S A CONCEPT.

The visual design, responsive pages, interaction logic and browser-local sample state are coded. The model, brand, debit card, balances, users and transaction history are fictional. Transfers, payments, savings and card freezing do not contact banks, card networks, M-Pesa or external services. The investment and business ideas are demonstrations only; they are not financial offers.

Photographs are real editorial examples provided by their photographers on Unsplash; they illustrate a fictional campaign and do not show YELO customers or imply an endorsement. YELO is an independently named concept. It is not Co-op Bank's official YEA application, nor is it commissioned by the bank.
