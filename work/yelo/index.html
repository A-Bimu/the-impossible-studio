We studied Co-operative Bank's Young Ennovators Account (YEA), a proposition for youth aged 18–35. Its published capabilities already include online KES/USD account opening, transfers, bills, goal-based savings, virtual cards, lending services, and investments from KSh 100. The minimum published account-opening balance is KSh 500. This is an existing banking product, not a blank slate.

YELO is an independently named speculative design, not a Co-op Bank redesign or official app. Spending clarity, editorial presentation and a simplified activity-first interface are our design proposals—not claims about features missing from the bank. Read the official YEA account information ↗

The design question became: how do we make an app feel young without making money harder to manage?

Everyday is the product.

Put transfers, bill payments, balance visibility and sample transaction feedback ahead of decorative lifestyle tiles.

Progress is visible.

A fictional laptop fund tracks what you've set aside. The product does the simple math and shows it clearly.

Art is not an interface.

The ink and paint live on the card and campaign. Forms, numbers and financial states use a quiet, disciplined hierarchy.

NOT
ANOTHER
TEMPLATE.

We moved away from the first version's familiar purple gradients, decorative floating elements, repetitive rounded feature cards and emoji-like icons.

The replacement borrows from real streetwear editorial practice: authentic portraiture, unbalanced composition, concise type, generous open space and an imperfect human energy. The story reads like a small fashion publication, while the banking UI looks like software someone could actually use.

THE CARD
IS THE
CANVAS.

The front of this debit-card concept uses an original hand-built graffiti composition beneath a glossy laminate. A realistic metal chip, thickness, glare and magnetic stripe make it feel like a tangible object; mouse tilt and a front/back flip bring it to life.

BUILT TO CLICK.
NOT TO MISLEAD.

The website, card rendering and five banking demo journeys are implemented in the browser. Send sample funds, pay a fictional bill, save towards a goal, inspect activity and freeze a mock card. These operations work against browser-local sample state. There is no bank account, real card issuer, M-Pesa API, payment processing, credit product, or real customer data.

All photographs are real editorial images by Dwayne joe under the Unsplash License; pictured people are not customers or endorsers of YELO. Images were not generated to resemble real customers. No business was commissioned to create this concept. Research background: Co-op Bank personal banking and mobile banking.
