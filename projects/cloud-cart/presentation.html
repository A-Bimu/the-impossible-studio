Cloud Cart.

A curated online store for everyday groceries, household essentials and local favourites. The concept pairs a warm, imaginative campaign with familiar, practical shopping flows.

Explore the live demo ↗
Concept

Good things float.

The little cloud carrying a shopping basket turns routine grocery shopping into a lighter, more joyful moment. This is a direct-to-customer online retail concept, not a seller marketplace or courier service.

Visual direction

Sunset, sky, warmth, confidence.

Peach and cloud-cream backgrounds, clear charcoal typography, warm orange actions, and blue skies. Interactive prototype uses sample products and simulates checkout only.
