WHY THIS EXISTS

FARMING IS
ENOUGH WORK.

When leaves change colour, the rains shift or a buyer makes an offer, getting the right information should be easier than guessing.

AGRI AI is designed around the questions farmers already ask, with plain-language guidance and space for local extension expertise.

CROP HEALTH / EXAMPLE

What's happening to my crop?

Upload a photo, describe visible symptoms and get a transparent, illustrative next-step checklist—not a pretend diagnosis.

SEASON / EXAMPLE

What needs doing next?

Build a simple planning checklist by crop, county and stage. Save progress on your own device.

MARKETS / EXAMPLE

Does this offer make sense?

Work out potential gross sale value with your actual quantity and independently confirmed price.

SOMEWHERE BETWEEN SOIL AND SOFTWARE

REAL FIELDS.
REAL QUESTIONS.

The design starts with what is actually happening in a field: soil conditions, crop symptoms, stage of growth, available inputs and access to reliable people.

Technology should make those details understandable, without hiding uncertainty behind a big AI badge.
