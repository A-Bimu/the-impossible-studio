DON'T
DO PLAIN.

A card can look like something worth keeping without losing the stuff that makes it useful.

Purple, paint, an orange slash, and a deliberately loud logo. The artwork is real graffiti-inspired product design, with a reflective plastic finish and physically believable depth. Hover to tilt and tap to flip. Nothing here is a real issued debit card.

THE MARK
IS THE POINT.

The graffiti isn't behind the bank. It's on the card itself. An original vector mark sits beneath a reflective laminate, visible chip, embossed-style text and realistic edges. Move your cursor over it or flip to the back.

TRY CARD CONTROLS ↗

LOUD LOOK.
QUIET LOGIC.

We deliberately separate decoration from financial information. The user shouldn't need to read through artwork to check a status or freeze the card.

BRAND MARKS

Original painted strokes and a large display logo. A distinctive look that isn't a generic gradient or a borrowed brand identity.

SAFE DEFAULTS

The number is masked, the label says “demo,” and there are no claims of bank issuance, card-network membership or purchase capability.

CARD CONTROL

A working freeze/unfreeze toggle shows how the product could behave. It updates browser-local state, not any real payment service.
