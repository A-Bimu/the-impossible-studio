WHY STREET CULTURE / AN EDITORIAL POINT OF VIEW

THE
PEOPLE
FIRST.

This isn't a costume made of spray-paint textures. The people, places and ideas come before the effects.

Our reference is the way local style makes space for individuality. We paired photographs genuinely taken in Nairobi with carefully placed original marks, readable banking information and a bit of attitude.

LESS FILTER.
MORE FEELING.

We removed the artificially glossy ingredients: generic emoji icons, identical cards, excessive gradients and big vague claims. Street culture is a visual point of view, not a Photoshop effect.

01 /

USE REAL PORTRAITS

Actual photographs by a credited Nairobi photographer anchor the brand. The pictured individuals are not fictional YELO customers.

02 /

LET TYPE DO THE WORK

Condensed, recognisable display type carries the voice. Small, plain typography carries the money information.

03 /

RESPECT THE SPACE

Spray marks belong on the card and a few campaign surfaces. Not on the balances, payment fields or every section of the website.

A POINT OF VIEW / 01

NOT AN
ASSET PACK.

Editorial photography isn't there to fill an empty half of a hero. It establishes who the concept speaks to and grounds the visual language in a real context.

A POINT OF VIEW / 02

SPACE TO
BE YOURSELF.

Money can be serious without looking like the same bank app as everyone else's. We use the confidence of clothing campaigns, then strip the interface back to the tasks users need.
