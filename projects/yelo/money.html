A PRACTICAL BANKING CONCEPT / NO LIVE TRANSACTIONS

MONEY
WITHOUT
THE NOISE.

A quick payment shouldn't feel like a puzzle. Nor should saving for something that matters.

YELO starts with ordinary tasks: a balance you can read, buttons you can find, clear action feedback, and a goal you can keep coming back to. This is a locally simulated product experience.

ONE APP.
THREE MOMENTS.

What happens when the website follows real situations instead of another grid of promises? You get a clear beginning, middle and end.

01 /

IT'S PAYDAY.

See your available sample balance immediately. Add fictional funds if you want to explore the transfers. No account numbers or bank credentials requested.

02 /

RENT'S DUE.

Enter a fictional recipient and an amount. Check the sample transaction feedback and recent activity. No M-Pesa or bank integration is connected.

03 /

NEW LAPTOP?

Put aside KSh 500 or another sample amount. The money moves from your demo balance to the goal, and the progress bar changes.

THE BORING
STUFF MATTERS.

01
Show the amount.

Real numbers, clear currency, and an honest balance after a simulated transaction.

02
Explain the action.

Use familiar language—send, bills, save—rather than banking acronyms.

03
Don't pretend.

Fees, loan eligibility and payment networks would require regulated infrastructure; none are simulated as official offers here.
