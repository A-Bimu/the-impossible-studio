Print and export / A4 portrait / 12 sheets

The editorial edition / 2027

Everything below is the approved 2027 artwork at full resolution: each sheet is one designed month. Nothing is redrawn, cropped or re-lettered for print.

  1. Press Print / save as PDF (or Ctrl/Cmd + P).
  2. Choose paper size A4, orientation Portrait, margins None, and turn background graphics on.
  3. Each of the twelve designed pages then prints on its own sheet, ready to bind or to keep as a PDF.
