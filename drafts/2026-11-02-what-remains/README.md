# What Remains

**Status:** Draft for visual and interaction review. Not approved for publication, not linked from the Studio homepage.

**Date:** 2 November 2026  
**Medium:** Interactive erasure poetry, editorial layout, browser-native print  
**Audience:** Curious readers, poets and visitors who want to make something rather than just scroll.

## Central idea

A passage becomes a new poem by removing most of its words. The visitor chooses which words deserve to survive; the rest become physical-looking dark marks. The relationship is between editorial omission and personal authorship: absence is the material.

## Art direction

- **Reference medium:** Erasure/blackout poetry and book arts, not a simulated painting or physical print.
- **Composition:** Oversized asymmetric title, a paper-like reading sheet and a dark side rail for editing and the resulting poem.
- **Palette:** Lavender field, ivory sheet, midnight ink, coral selection and golden emphasis. This is intentionally distinct from the two-ink poster and record-sleeve studies.
- **Type:** System serif for literary material; plain sans-serif for controls. No third-party fonts or stock assets.
- **Interaction:** Select individual words using pointer or keyboard; preview the erasure, return to editing, reset, and print an A4 page.
- **Progressive enhancement:** The complete original passage remains readable with JavaScript disabled. No data is stored or transmitted.

## Original text and rights

The three-paragraph passage and the page implementation were created for this draft. No external images, libraries, audio or fonts are embedded. This is a code-built digital composition, not a photograph of a real print.
