A passage for disappearing / Original text

The things we almost said

At the edge of the city, a window held the last square of daylight. Someone had left a chair facing the street, as though the evening might arrive with news.

The buses carried their small bright rooms through the rain. On the pavement, a paper flower turned slowly in a puddle, refusing to become ordinary.

By morning, the streets would have other names for everything. For now there was only the light, the waiting, and the quiet courage of staying.
