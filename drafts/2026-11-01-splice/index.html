The idea behind the work

Not a picture
of a product.

SPLICE is a working piece of graphic design: editable type, original vector geometry and three deliberate compositional systems. Collision overlaps two opposing rhythms. Fold borrows from cut-paper sleeve art. Frequency turns repetition into a visual beat. Exported SVGs preserve shapes and text for further editing in vector software.
